💡 How Friend Calling Works:
  1. Sign in with your Gmail (e.g. signkipdesign@gmail.com).
  2. Open an Incognito tab or mobile phone, and sign in with another Gmail (or Demo account Anjali).
  3. In the search box above, enter the other person's Gmail address (e.g. anjali.demo@gmail.com) and click ➕ Add Friend.
  4. The other account receives a Friend Request. Once they click Accept, both of you appear in each other's Friends List!
  5. Click 📞 Call next to their name in the Friends List to start talking!
